Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Versión en español

ALAVIAN CLIPS ("we", "the service") is a web application available at https://alavianclips.duckdns.org that helps creators turn long videos and livestreams into short clips and, optionally, publish them to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the service handles.

Data we collect

  • Account data: the username and password you choose to sign in to the service. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • TikTok data (only if you connect TikTok through Login Kit): your TikTok open ID, display name, and the access and refresh tokens needed to publish on your behalf. We request only the permissions needed for this (user.info.basic and video.publish / video.upload). We do not read your private messages, followers or any other data.
  • Content you provide: videos, links or livestreams you choose to process, and the clips generated from them, including titles, captions and hashtags.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, create clips, and publish them to your own TikTok account only when you press Publish.
  • We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it except as described below.

Third-party services

  • TikTok: to sign you in and publish the videos you choose.
  • AI providers (Groq and Google Gemini): audio, transcripts or text from the content you process may be sent to them to detect highlights, transcribe speech and suggest titles.

Retention and deletion

  • Generated clips are deleted automatically from our server: 30 minutes after creation if they were not published, and about 20 minutes after you publish them or mark them as uploaded.
  • You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from the app; this deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access from your TikTok security settings.
  • To delete your account and data, write to sparkmatos71@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Security

The service is served over HTTPS and access to the app requires signing in. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
